Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Information we collect

When you sign in to ReadyAd, we collect the following information from your chosen sign-in provider (Google or GitHub):

  • Your email address
  • Your name and profile photo

When you use the ad generator, we also collect the product details you type into the form — such as your product name, target audience, age range, tone, language, website link, and main benefit — in order to generate ad copy for you.

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Sign you in and keep you signed in across sessions
  • Generate Google Ads copy (headlines, descriptions, keywords, and poster ideas) based on the product details you provide

Third-party services

ReadyAd uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — handles authentication (sign-in, sign-up, and session management). Your email and profile information are processed by Supabase to keep you logged in.
  • Google Gemini — generates the ad copy from the product details you submit. The text you type is sent to Google Gemini to produce headlines, descriptions, keywords, and poster ideas.

Guest usage

If you use ReadyAd without signing in, a guest usage counter is stored in your browser's local storage (under the key readyad-guest-count). This counter tracks how many free ads you have generated (guests get 3 free ads). This data stays in your browser and is not sent to our servers. Clearing your browser's local storage resets the counter.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect payment information. We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.
