        How it works

        ReadyAd makes creating Google Ads simple. Fill in a few quick details about your product, audience, and tone, then click Create my ad. In about 30 seconds, ReadyAd generates ad titles, descriptions, search words, and a poster idea you can copy and use.

        Who it is for

        Founders, freelancers and small business owners who need a free Google Ads generator for quick ad copy. No agency, no design tools and no ad account required.

        What is a free Google Ads generator?

        A free Google Ads generator is a tool that writes your Google Ads copy for you: the short headlines, the longer descriptions and the search keywords, without charging per ad, per seat or per campaign. Instead of staring at a blank box and guessing what fits inside Google's character limits, you describe your product in one line and the tool hands back copy that is ready to paste. It works as a Google Ads copy generator when you need the text, and as an ad headline generator when you need ten versions of the same idea.

        ReadyAd is one of these tools, and it is free in the plain sense. There is no account, no card and no trial timer. You open the page, describe what you sell, and the ad copy appears in about 30 seconds.

        How ReadyAd turns a product name into ad copy

        Everything ReadyAd needs, it asks in plain language. You type what you are selling, for example an insulated water bottle, then choose who will buy it, an age range and a tone such as friendly, premium or urgent. That is the whole input.

        From those details ReadyAd returns 10 ad headlines, each kept under 30 characters so it fits the Google Ads headline field, 4 descriptions, each a complete sentence under 90 characters, 8 Google Ads keyword ideas grouped by theme, and a poster idea for the image. Every line has its own Copy button, so you can move a headline or a keyword into your campaign without retyping it. If you would rather work in a spreadsheet, the CSV download gives you the headlines, descriptions and keywords in one file.

        Because the copy is written from your product name and audience instead of a fixed template, it sounds like your business. A coffee shop gets warm, local lines; a skincare brand gets calmer, ingredient-led ones.

        Why small businesses use an AI ad generator

        Most small businesses do not have a copywriter on call, and paying an agency for one search campaign is rarely worth it. An AI ad generator removes that bottleneck. You get a first draft in half a minute at no cost, and you can run it again whenever you launch a product or a seasonal offer.

        The practical gains are variety and speed. Ten headlines and four descriptions give you enough material to test three or four combinations in Google Ads instead of guessing one. The keyword list is a starting point for your keyword field, and it is honest about its limits: it gives you Google Ads keyword ideas, not search volumes, so check demand in Google Keyword Planner before you commit budget.

        ReadyAd also writes in English, Hindi and Hinglish, which matters when your customers search in more than one language. You can run the same product in two languages and compare the lines side by side.

        What ReadyAd does not do

        ReadyAd writes copy. It does not connect to your Google Ads account, spend your budget or publish a campaign for you. You paste the lines into Google Ads yourself, which also means nothing goes live without your review. The safety helper on the results screen flags lines that may need a check, but it is a quick helper, not an official Google review.

        Help / FAQ

        FAQ

        Is ReadyAd really free?

        Yes. There is no account, no credit card and no per-ad charge. You can generate, copy and download as many ads as you need.

        How many headlines can I generate?

        Each run gives you 10 headlines, 4 descriptions, 8 keywords and a poster idea. Run it again with a different tone or audience to get a fresh set.

        Do I need a Google Ads account to use ReadyAd?

        No. ReadyAd only writes the copy. You paste the headlines and descriptions into Google Ads yourself when you are ready to launch.

        Can it write ads in Hindi or Hinglish?

        Yes. Choose Hindi or Hinglish in the form and the headlines, descriptions and keywords are written in that language, within the same character limits.

        Where do the keyword ideas come from?

        They come from your product name, audience and tone, and they are grouped by theme so you can see which ideas belong together. Treat them as a starting point and check search demand in Google Keyword Planner before you spend.
